# **01. FOUNDER STORY**

I grew up in a small town — the kind of place where standing out wasn't celebrated.

But even then, something in me was searching.

I didn't have the language for it yet. I didn't have the references, the city, or the context. What I had was a feeling — that the way you dressed could say something true about who you were, before you ever opened your mouth.

So I'd wander into stores my friends never set foot in. I'd put together things in ways that felt right to me, even when no one around me understood why.

It started in a place called Sylvan Hills.

That name has always meant something to me — not just as a place, but as a feeling. The feeling of stepping away from the noise. Of going somewhere quiet enough to hear yourself think. Of finding, beneath all the expectation and influence, something that was just yours.

Then came New York. Then came the world.

I traveled. I observed. I absorbed. I stood in cities where men dressed with a kind of effortless authority I'd never seen in a small town — where a jacket wasn't a jacket, it was a statement of self. Where restraint was more powerful than flash. Where the most compelling men in the room were often the quietest ones.

And I kept wanting things slightly different. Slightly more considered. The right fabric, but with more ease. The right structure, but without the stiffness. I could always see what something could be — and that gap never left me.

Sylvan Hills is that gap, finally closed.

It is the brand I would have wanted to discover at 22. The one that would have told me: you don't have to perform. You don't have to follow. You just have to know who you are — and dress from that place.

I'm still on that journey. I suspect I always will be.

But I know this:

*"The clothes we wear are a quiet declaration. And every piece I design is made for the man who's ready to make his."*

# **02. BRAND WORLDVIEW**

We live in a world that has confused dressing with performing.

Social media didn't create this problem — but it accelerated it. Today, men's wardrobes are increasingly curated by algorithms, trend cycles, and the anxiety of being seen. Style has become a transaction: wear this, get this reaction.

Sylvan Hills rejects that entirely.

We believe that the most powerful thing a man can wear is a quiet, unshakeable sense of himself. That real style isn't adopted — it's arrived at. Through living, traveling, failing, growing, and paying attention.

We believe clothing should serve the man, not define him. That a well-made garment is not a status symbol — it's a personal ritual. Something you reach for because it feels true, not because it's trending.

We believe in fewer things, made better. In fabrics that age gracefully. In silhouettes that don't apologize. In design that earns its details.

Sylvan Hills is for the man who has done the inner work — or is committed to doing it. Who moves through the world with intention, not performance. Who doesn't need to be the loudest presence in the room to be the most felt.

*"This is not a brand about fashion. This is a brand about becoming."*

# **04. BRAND PILLARS**

Every piece of content, every caption, every campaign, and every product decision should live inside one of these three pillars. They are your creative architecture — the repeatable framework that keeps Sylvan Hills coherent across every touchpoint.

## **Pillar I — The Return**
***Coming home to yourself***
This is the emotional territory Sylvan Hills owns. The idea that self-knowledge is the foundation of real style. Content here explores identity, authenticity, the journey of becoming, and what it means to dress without performing.

**Content examples:**
— The moment you stopped dressing for others
— What your wardrobe says about where you are in life
— Why the most powerful thing a man can wear is certainty

## **Pillar II — The Craft**
***Fewer things, made better***
This is the product integrity pillar. Fabric, construction, sourcing, design decisions, and the philosophy behind making something built to last. This pillar builds trust and justifies premium pricing.

**Content examples:**
— Why we chose this fabric over that one
— What makes a jacket feel grounded versus stiff
— The difference between a garment made fast and one made right

## **Pillar III — The Life**
***Living with intention***
This is the lifestyle and culture pillar. The world the Sylvan Hills man inhabits — travel, music, art, food, city, nature. Not aspirational fantasy. Grounded, real, quietly extraordinary.

**Content examples:**
— A morning in the city that reminded me why I started this brand
— The jacket worn on a first date, a dinner party, and a walk through the woods — all the same jacket
— What I learned about restraint from watching how other cultures dress

# **05. BRAND NARRATIVE**

There is a place called Sylvan Hills.

Not just on a map — though it exists there too, as the home where something in me first woke up. It's the feeling of stepping away from the noise long enough to hear yourself. Of stripping back the expectation, the influence, the performance — and finding, underneath all of it, something that is just yours.

I grew up in a small town with a feeling I couldn't explain. Something in me wanted to express who I was through what I wore — but I didn't have the language, the city, or the references. So I went to stores my friends ignored. I put things together in ways that made sense to me, even when no one else understood. I was searching for something, without knowing what it was called.

Then New York. Then the world.

Traveling changed the way I saw everything — especially men and how they dressed. I discovered what it looked like when a man wore something because it was true to him, not because it was trending. I stood in rooms where the most compelling presence was also the quietest. Where restraint said more than flash ever could. Where American ease and Italian sartorial discipline somehow coexisted in a single, perfect jacket.

And I kept seeing things I wanted to make slightly different. More considered. More grounded. The gap between what existed and what I knew was possible never left me.

Sylvan Hills is that gap, finally built.

This brand is for the man who has arrived — or is on his way. Who dresses not to be seen, but to be himself. Who invests in fewer things because he understands that quality is not a luxury, it's a practice. Who moves through the world with quiet confidence, knowing that the most powerful thing he can wear is his own unshakeable sense of self.

Our clothes are designed to go where he goes — from a first date to a dinner party, a city walk to a quiet morning, a meeting that matters to a night that does too. Not because they're versatile in the generic sense. Because they're intentional in the deepest sense.

We blend American ease with Italian restraint. We obsess over fabric, fit, and form. We make fewer things, and we make them better.

Sylvan Hills is more than a clothing brand.

It is a philosophy of presence. A declaration of self. A return to what lasts.

*"This is where you go when you're done performing — and ready to just be."*
