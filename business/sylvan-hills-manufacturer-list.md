## Questions to Ask in Manufacturer Meetings

1. What is your MOQ for woven shirts / casual trousers / outerwear separately?
2. Do you have experience with relaxed but tailored silhouettes (not activewear)?
3. Can you share examples of menswear you've produced in a similar aesthetic?
4. What is your typical sample-to-production turnaround?
5. Do you offer pattern development and tech pack support, or do I need to supply those?
6. What are your fabric sourcing capabilities (deadstock, domestics, Japanese mills)?
7. What does pricing look like at 50 units vs. 100 units vs. 200 units per style?
