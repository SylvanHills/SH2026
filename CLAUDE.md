# Sylvan Hills — Brand Context for Claude Code

## What is Sylvan Hills?

Sylvan Hills is an early-stage men's clothing brand founded by Garrett, currently based in Los Angeles. The brand focuses on apparel including shirts, jackets, pants, hats, sweaters, and t-shirts. The core design philosophy combines relaxed silhouettes with disciplined tailoring — garments that feel easy to wear but are built with intention and craft.

## Brand Positioning

Sylvan Hills targets men who value both comfort and quality construction. The brand sits in the premium-accessible space, not fast fashion and not luxury — think thoughtful, wearable pieces with longevity. Competitive reference points include Todd Snyder, Engineered Garments, and Auralee.
