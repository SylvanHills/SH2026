# CLAUDE.md — Sylvan Hills Brand Context
## Who You Are Working With
Sylvan Hills is a men's clothing brand founded by Garrett, based in
Los Angeles. The brand is in early-stage development with a target
launch in 2026. Garrett is currently transitioning out of his current
job within 6 months to run Sylvan Hills full time.
## Brand Purpose & Philosophy
Sylvan Hills exists for men who have outgrown the need to perform
through their clothing. The brand rejects performance-driven dressing
in favor of garments that feel like a return — to ease, to craft, to
a life lived on your own terms. The core design philosophy combines
relaxed silhouettes with disciplined tailoring: garments that are
intentionally at ease, never accidentally sloppy.
## The Two Anchor Lines
These are the brand's core message. Use them to pressure-test all
content and documentation for consistency:
- **Brand Statement:** "Sylvan Hills is where you go when you're done performing and ready to just be."
- **Product Positioning Line:** "Clothing for the man who dresses from the inside out."
## The Three Brand Pillars
All content, product decisions, and brand communications should
connect back to one or more of these pillars:
- **The Return** — Coming home to yourself. The emotional territory: self-knowledge as the foundation of real style, identity, authenticity, dressing without performing.
- **The Craft** — Fewer things, made better. Product integrity: fabric, construction, sourcing, design philosophy, building trust, justifying premium pricing.
- **The Life** — Living with intention. Lifestyle and culture: the world the Sylvan Hills man inhabits — travel, music, art, city, nature. Grounded, not aspirational fantasy.
## Target Customer — Three Archetypes
Reference these when making product, content, or positioning decisions:
- **Everett, 26** — A creative tastemaker developing his own eye. Experimenting, but already knows he doesn't want to look like everyone else. Sylvan Hills helps him solidify his identity with quiet confidence.
- **Nick, 35** — A founder or self-made professional. Life is full; wardrobe needs to be simple, elevated, and intentional. Sylvan Hills gives him pieces that do the thinking for him.
- **Kwame, 48** — A man who has already arrived. Values timelessness, craftsmanship, and restraint. Wears Sylvan Hills not to be noticed — but because it feels right.
## Brand Personality & Voice
- Tone: Confident without being arrogant. Founder-voiced, not corporate.
- Writing style: Direct, purposeful, unhurried. Reflects the brand's
  rejection of performance and noise.
- Never: Hype-driven, trend-chasing, or overly polished marketing speak.
## Aesthetic & Positioning
- Aesthetic: Relaxed silhouettes, disciplined construction, quality
  materials, timeless over trendy
- Market position: Premium-accessible. Not fast fashion, not luxury.
  Wearable pieces built with intention and longevity.
- Competitive references: Todd Snyder, Engineered Garments, Auralee
