**Sylvan Hills – Launch Product Thesis**
**Working Title:** *The** “Threshold”(cross the threshold into Sylvan Hills) or “Overlook” (childhood street name / meaningful / the beginning)** Jacket*

**1. The Cultural Problem We’re Solving**
Men’s fashion is split between two extremes:
Fashion-forward pieces that feel unapproachable, impractical, or performative
Utility garments that are functional but lack soul, clean lines
Most jackets either **say too much** or **say nothing at all**.
They don’t feel designed for a man who wants to move through the world with intention — not chasing trends, not hiding behind basics.
We believe a jacket can be both **elevated and lived in**.
It can express individuality *without noise*.

**2. The Man We Are Designing For**
The man who wears this jacket:
Is comfortable in his own skin
Trusts his taste over algorithms
Values quality over volume
Moves easily between worlds — creative, social, professional
Believes clothes should support his life, not perform for others
He wants to feel **composed, grounded, and quietly confident** — without trying to be seen.

**3. The Garment Archetype**
Our first product is a **hybrid jacket** that merges:
The **Nehru jacket silhouette** — refined, architectural, modern. First saw / inspired when traveling through India. Want to bring that culture and global mindset into the garment, bridging worlds.
The **chore jacket** — relaxed, functional, lived-in.
This is not outerwear.
It is a **daily uniform layer**.
Something he reaches for without thinking — because it always feels right.

**4. The Functional Promise**
This jacket must:
Move seamlessly from day → night
Balance structure with comfort
Feel light yet substantial
Carry essentials without bulk
Age beautifully with wear
Eye catching but subtle
It should feel like a **second skin** — not a costume.

**5. The Emotional Promise**
When he puts it on, he should feel:
**Grounded**
**Capable**
**Quietly exceptional**
Not louder.
Just more himself.

**6. The Differentiation Axis**
This is not just a jacket.
It is a **philosophical uniform** for men who choose presence over performance.
It stands at the intersection of:
**Modern tailoring × Everyday utility**
Nothing decorative.
Nothing accidental.

**7. Price & Position**
**Modern Heritage / Premium Casual**
Target Retail: **$195–$325**
The quality must feel *wrong* at a lower price.
If this were sold cheaper, it would betray the intention.

**8. Why This Is the Right First Product**
This jacket:
Encapsulates the brand philosophy
Is instantly recognizable
Can anchor future collections
Sparks curiosity and conversation
Feels like a signature
If this fails, the brand does not deserve to exist.

**9. One-Sentence Thesis**
**We launch with a hybrid Nehru–chore jacket that helps men feel quietly confident, because we believe clothing should liberate rather than conform.**
