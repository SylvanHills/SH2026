Sylvan Hills exists for men who dress from quiet self-assurance, not external validation. We create intentional, grounded garments that merge American ease with Italian restraint — timeless pieces for men who have their own taste.

**Category:** Hybrid Outerwear (Nehru × Chore Jacket)

### The Cultural Problem We're Solving

> **[MERGED — Section from draft; the final replaced this with a shorter "Customer Problem" framing. Both are preserved as they complement each other.]**
>
> Men's fashion is split between two extremes:
> - Fashion-forward pieces that feel unapproachable, impractical, or performative
> - Utility garments that are functional but lack soul, clean lines
>
> Most jackets either **say too much** or **say nothing at all.**
> They don't feel designed for a man who wants to move through the world with intention — not chasing trends, not hiding behind basics.
>
> We believe a jacket can be both **elevated and lived in.** It can express individuality *without noise.*

### Customer Problem

Men with developed taste face a persistent wardrobe gap: they want outerwear that feels distinct without being costume-like, and casual without being careless.

Existing options force a choice:
- Nehru jackets feel too formal, too "occasion-specific"
- Chore jackets feel too workwear, too ubiquitous

The Threshold Jacket solves this by merging the unique distinction of Nehru styling with the everyday utility of chore jacket functionality — creating a third-piece layer that works across contexts without compromise.

### The Man We Are Designing For

The man who wears this jacket:
- Is comfortable in his own skin
- Trusts his taste over algorithms
- Values quality over volume
- Moves easily between worlds — creative, social, professional
- Believes clothes should support his life, not perform for others

He wants to feel **composed, grounded, and quietly confident** — without trying to be seen.

Our first product is a **hybrid jacket** that merges:

### Price & Position

**Expected Retail Price: $450–$650**

**Price Rationale:**
- Cut-and-sew construction with premium natural fabrics (Italian or Japanese cotton/linen blends)
- Unique hybrid silhouette — not available from any competitor at any price
- Positioning against: Officine Générale ($400–$700), Private White V.C. ($450–$800), Corridor ($300–$500)
- Not competing with: Everlane, Uniqlo, or commodity outerwear brands

The price reflects:
- Manufacturing reality (premium fabric + custom pattern + quality construction)
- Emotional value (ownable design that changes how he occupies space)
- Brand positioning (Sylvan Hills is not "affordable basics" — it's intentional investment)

## Post-Launch Expansion (Foundation Products)

Once the Threshold Jacket has established brand credibility and customer trust, Sylvan Hills will introduce:
- **The Essential Tee** ($75–$95) — substantial layering piece
- **The Foundation Trouser** ($185–$240) — refined casual bottoms

These products will be marketed as "Complete the Look" — not as standalone heroes, but as natural complements to the jacket.

**Strategic Intent:** Allow customers to build a Sylvan Hills wardrobe after the jacket has earned their trust and defined what the brand represents.
